# On the Record: PNN and BULL

The press briefing gives each network a headline and a reporter question. PNN focuses on evidence, accountability, and international consequences. BULL has an explicitly conservative editorial voice: taxpayer costs, private-sector jobs, local control, public safety, and national sovereignty.

Both voices are generated in the existing staff request, using campaign history, recorded decisions, PNN bulletins, and BULL local facts. No additional model request is needed. They cannot create events or executable actions. Offline or older briefings use deterministic questions derived from the same saved world.

“Answer” opens the Press Secretary and copies the question into an editable announcement draft. The player adds their answer and publishes through the existing daily announcement flow. This records the exchange and supplies it to subsequent character and social reactions. The existing one-announcement-per-day rule applies; answering does not enact policy. Bill introduction, negotiation, and votes remain available below the questions.

No world schema or replay format changed. Tests cover media validation, safe rendering, local-news context, fallback questions, and recording/replaying a public answer.
