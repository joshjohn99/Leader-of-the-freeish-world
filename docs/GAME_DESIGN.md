# Game design

## Vision

Play a fictional president in a satirical world where another country's decisions change your options, citizens react through a phone and social feed, and national conditions eventually change the visible environment.

Government, citizens, and billionaires form competing sources of power. Countries depend on each other for resources, making cooperation useful and betrayal costly.

## Core loop

Read a developing situation, choose a policy or negotiate, observe consequences across countries, respond to public reaction, and make the next decision under changed conditions.

## First prototype

Three fictional nations: Freedomia, Petrovia, and Technovia (working names). Start with text output and explicit turns. Add one billionaire after the country rules work. Later connect a small presidential office, character, and phone in Unreal.

## Direction still to test

PC/Steam is the intended release platform. Multiplayer starts with two players. A finite World Era is a candidate session structure; its duration, scoring, victory conditions, and persistence remain design decisions to playtest. AI-generated satire enriches consequences but cannot replace the underlying rules.

## Outside the first milestone

Open worlds, crowds, combat, mobile companion apps, production multiplayer, paid AI generation, and persistent database infrastructure.
